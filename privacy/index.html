Privacy Policy

Effective November 6, 2026

In short: Daily Buddha does not collect, share or sell any of your personal information. There is no account, no advertising and no tracking. What the app remembers about you stays on your iPhone.

This policy explains how the Daily Buddha app for iPhone ("the app"), made by Amos Live, Inc. ("we", "us"), handles information.

Information we collect

None. The app does not send any information about you or how you use it to us or to anyone else. We do not use analytics, advertising, crash reporting or tracking tools, and the app contains no third‑party code that collects data.

Information kept on your iPhone

To work, the app saves a small amount of information on your iPhone only:

  • Your first name, if you choose to enter one, so the app can greet you.
  • Your streak, the teachings you have received and the dates you received them.
  • Your reminder settings, such as your morning reminder time.

We cannot see this information. It is included in your iPhone's own iCloud or computer backups if you use them, which are handled by Apple. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

Reminders

If you allow notifications, the app schedules its morning and evening reminders on your iPhone. They are not sent from a server, and no information leaves your device to deliver them. You can turn reminders off at any time in the iPhone Settings app under Notifications.

Subscriptions and payments

Subscriptions are sold and managed by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your name, email address, payment card or Apple Account details. The app only asks Apple whether a subscription is active. Apple's handling of your purchase is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

The voice

Each teaching was recorded with an AI‑generated voice before the app was released, and the recordings are included in the app. Nothing is generated while you use the app, and nothing you say or do in the app is recorded or sent anywhere, to an AI service or to anyone else. The app does not use your microphone.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and because it collects no personal information, it does not knowingly collect information from children.

Your choices and rights

Because we hold no personal information about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or delete. You stay in control of everything the app saves: you can change your first name in the app's Settings, and deleting the app removes all of its information from your iPhone.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles information, we will update this page and the date at the top before the change takes effect. If the app ever begins to collect information, we will explain what and why here first.

Contact us

Questions about privacy are welcome at support@amos.live.
